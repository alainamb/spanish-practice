
Activity Introduction
Vocabulary challenge: How many -erías can I find on a street in Mexico City? 
The suffix -ería is used to convert a noun or a verb into a shop that sells the related goods and services.

For example:

dulces (sweets/candy) → dulcería (candy shop)
tacos → taquería (with a spelling change from "c" to "qu" to hold the "hard c" sound)

Any my favorite:

verduras (veggies) → verdulería (with the second "r" from verduras being replaced with an "l" to avoid "-rería" at the end of this word, though -erías don't always avoid the -rería pattern)

To help you learn this pattern, we invite you to take a virtual stroll, drive, bikeride, whatever you prefer to call it, down Avenida Centenario, identifying -erías and other places as you go.

Avenida Centenario, in Mexico City, is a street with a lot of -erías. Some time back, we found 23 -erías on this street.

Instructions
Starting from the Google Streetview embedded below, go for a virtual "walk/bikeride/drive" on Avenida Centennario. Keep a list of the -erías and the other places you find there.
